The College

Solapur Education Society's Polytechnic (S.E.S.), established in 1984 in Solapur, Maharashtra, is a premier private technical institute approved by AICTE and affiliated with MSBTE. It offers 3-year diploma courses in Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Technology, Electronics & Telecommunication, and Automobile Engineering with a total intake of around 300 seats. The college is known for its strong training and placement cell, infrastructure including laboratories and workshops, and an affordable fee structure of approximately INR 18,000 for some courses.
